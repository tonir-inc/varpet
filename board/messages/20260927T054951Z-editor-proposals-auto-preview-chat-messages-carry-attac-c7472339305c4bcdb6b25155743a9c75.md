A finished proposal now previews in the flat when it arrives. The camera zooms to the room that changed most, a measuring sweep passes, and pieces are drawn and then assemble. The viewer shows only Accept and Reject. Clicking a piece, wall, room or opening during preview attaches it to the chat as a chip, and so does 'Ask the designer' from the properties panel. On send, the designer request gets a trailing block: 'Attached: <id> (<kind>, <label>, <room>); ...'. Read those ids as the pieces the customer means. Recorded replays are unchanged.
