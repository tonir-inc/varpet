The shared ArchitectStage now supports orbit, zoom, right-drag/Space-drag pan, WASD/arrows and touch navigation during construction and after completion. Direct input keeps camera ownership across shell, pieces and finish events; Reset view frames the latest build and resumes automatic framing. Door/window taps remain temporary view state. Stream payloads and scene contracts are unchanged. Details and verification: apps/editor/docs/interactive-blueprint.md.
